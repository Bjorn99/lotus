Lotus 1.9.1

Swipe the album art left or right to change track, contributed by bxdxnn. Swiping right works like the skip-back button, so more than three seconds into a track it restarts that track.

Now fully translated into Turkish, with French underway.

Also fixed:
- Tracks longer than an hour showed a wrong time
- Track info showed no hours for long tracks
- A crash that could close the app at startup
- The player's top row of buttons hid behind the album art on small screens
