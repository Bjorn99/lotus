<b>Features</b>
<ul><li>Enjoy your music in MP3, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, WAV, M4A, and more</li>
<li>No ads, no telemetry, no analytics — ever</li>
<li>Network access is off by default and must be turned on in Settings</li>
<li>Smart Shuffle — Pure (mathematically unbiased) and Smart (avoids repeating artists)</li>
<li>Per-track embedded artwork — show the cover stored inside each file</li>
<li>Synchronized lyrics from LRCLIB with karaoke-style timed display</li>
<li>Manual lyrics import with automatic charset detection (UTF-8 and Latin-1)</li>
<li>Browse tracks, albums, artists, and genres; create and manage playlists</li>
<li>Smart Playlists — Recently Added and Random Mix, always up to date</li>
<li>Export playlists to M3U files for use in other music apps</li>
<li>Global search across tracks, albums, artists, genres, and playlists</li>
<li>Loved tracks — mark favorites and browse them in one place</li>
<li>Listening Stats — most played, most listened, recently played, top artists</li>
<li>Backup and restore playlists and loved tracks to a JSON file</li>
<li>Sleep timer with presets (15/30/45/60/90 min) and finish-current-track</li>
<li>Quick Settings tile for play/pause</li>
<li>Edit metadata tags or fetch accurate info from MusicBrainz</li>
<li>OPUS metadata editing — title, album, artist, genre, year, track number, lyrics</li>
<li>Share tracks via the system share sheet</li>
<li>Crash logs saved locally — share them manually, nothing is sent automatically</li>
<li>Designed with Material You and dynamic color palettes</li></ul>

All music stays on your device. Network access is off by default and must be turned on in Settings — LRCLIB lyrics lookups and MusicBrainz metadata queries are opt-in only.
