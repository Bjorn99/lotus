The Architectural Principle

Container sets screen-edge boundaries. Items define internal layout.

Tier 1 — Container (grid)

contentPadding = PaddingValues(horizontal = 16.dp, vertical = 8.dp)

Single source of truth for where content sits relative to the screen edge. Applies to ALL tabs identically. No exceptions.

Tier 2 — Items (content)

Items span column-to-column. Internal spacing (between text and icons, between cover art and labels) is the item's responsibility. Items do NOT add their own screen-edge margin.

The Fix

Grid container (PlayerScreen.kt:1529): Add top padding. Keep horizontal 16dp.

contentPadding = PaddingValues(
  horizontal = 16.dp,
  vertical = 8.dp
)

Result: ALL tabs = 16dp from screen edge. Zero exceptions.
